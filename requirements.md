The assignment
Your mission
Create an LLM agent to solve a novel problem of your choice.
It can be anything, but it should be interesting.
We recommend spending no more than 3 hours — the important part here
is how you think, not if you have tons of time to pour into a take-home
project.
While not mandatory (you build whatever you like), we’d expect the agent

Some other interesting innovation
Model choice
We will provide an OpenAI API key for your use. While you are free to use
any model, we recommend using a high-capability / frontier model to
ensure your agent has sufficient reasoning and tool-use ability. If you
choose to use a smaller or local model, please be prepared to discuss how
its limitations may have impacted your design and results.
Scope and expectations
This is a time-boxed exercise, and we do not expect production-ready
code. However, we do expect a working, end-to-end prototype that we can
interact with during the interview. As you scope your work, prioritize a
simple, well-functioning solution over a more complex system that may be
only partially complete. Clear thinking, good trade-offs, and a reliable demo
are more valuable than breadth of features.
